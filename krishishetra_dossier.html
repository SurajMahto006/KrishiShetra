🏛️ 3. Comprehensive Judge Q&A Defense Bank

Q1: "Government already built e-NAM. Why do we need KrishiShetra? How are you different?"
Market & Ground
Evaluator Intent: Testing if you understand e-NAM's actual ground friction vs theoretical premise.
Direct Answer: e-NAM is a visionary policy tool, but on the ground it suffers from 3 major operational bottlenecks that KrishiShetra specifically solves:
  1. Pre-Transport Listing vs Mandi Yard Queue: e-NAM still requires farmers to physically transport crops to the APMC mandi yard before bidding starts, incurring locked-in transport costs. KrishiShetra enables farm-gate advance listing before spending a single rupee on freight.
  2. Dynamic Multi-Turn Counter Negotiation: e-NAM relies on rigid single-round closed bids. KrishiShetra features an interactive counter-offer negotiation room where terms, price, and delivery timelines are refined collaboratively.
  3. Integrated Post-Harvest Decision Intelligence: e-NAM never advises a farmer whether to sell or store. KrishiShetra calculates biological moisture loss, warehouse rent, and seasonal price trajectories to give a quantified ROI recommendation.
Q2: "Traditional middlemen (Arhtiyas) lend cash to farmers. How will you get farmers to switch?"
Agrifinance
Evaluator Intent: Testing your knowledge of the informal credit trap tying farmers to commission agents.
Direct Answer: Middlemen retain farmers not through love, but through usurious credit (24%–36% p.a.) that forces pre-harvest crop commitments. We dismantle this loop through:
  • e-NWR Warehouse Pledge Financing: In PledgeFinancingRequest.js, farmers storing produce in accredited MSWC/CWC facilities immediately access 70%–75% pledge loan disbursals from partner financial institutions at 7%–9% subsidized agri-credit rates.
  • FPO Collective Shield: Aggregating smallholders via our fpo-dashboard.html allows group collateral and institutional buyer advance contracts.
Q3: "How does your AI 'Sell Now vs Store & Hold' Decision Engine calculate its recommendations?"
AI & Math
Evaluator Intent: Verifying if your AI is an actual algorithmic model or just arbitrary placeholder code.
Direct Answer: In server/services/decision.service.js, our engine evaluates:
Net Gain = Projected Future Realization - (Current Realization + Total Holding Costs)
It incorporates four physiological and economic parameters:
  • Storage Tariff: Exact facility rates (e.g., ₹38/bag/mo dry warehouse, ₹65/bag/mo cold chain).
  • Biological Weight & Moisture Shrinkage: Crop-specific loss models (Onion = 4.5%, Tomato = 3.8%, Potato = 2.2%, Wheat = 0.8% over 30–60 days).
  • Handling & Freight Delta: Loading charges (₹14/qtl) + distance to warehouse via Haversine calculation (₹14/km).
  • Threshold Gate: A "STORE & HOLD" recommendation is only triggered if net ROI is > +2.5%.
Q4: "How does the Mandi Price Arbitrage Engine compute freight and net profitability?"
Geospatial
Evaluator Intent: Testing geodesic math and real transport cost reality.
Direct Answer: In js/mandi-map.js and js/mandi-compare.js:
  • Calculates great-circle distance between farmer coordinates and 45+ APMC mandis using the Haversine Formula.
  • Applies dynamic rural vehicle freight curves: Transport Cost = Distance (km) × Qty (qtl) × ₹18–₹20/km.
  • Computes Net Realization = (Modal Mandi Price × Qty) - Freight Cost and automatically marks the highest net-profit market with a 🏆 Gold Trophy.

Q6: "How do you solve the Trust & Quality Assaying problem without seeing the crop?"
Quality & Trust
Evaluator Intent: Understanding dispute mitigation and remote commodity trade risks.
Direct Answer: We enforce a 3-layer trust architecture:
  • AGMARK Grade Parameters: Mandatory lot attributes (Grade A/B/C, moisture %, harvest date, seed variety, visual photos).
  • Accredited Warehouse Assaying: When stored in partner facilities, government-certified lab assaying certificates are attached to the e-NWR lot.
  • 6-Stage Escrow Pipeline: Buyer funds are placed in escrow upon order confirmation and released to farmer/transporter only upon destination OTP delivery sign-off.
